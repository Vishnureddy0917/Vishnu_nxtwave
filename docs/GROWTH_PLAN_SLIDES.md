### 1. Target Audience
* **Cohort:** Final-year (2025) and pre-final year (2026) B.Tech students (CSE, IT, AI/DS, ECE).

* **Resume Gap:** Generic To-Do tutorials fail recruiter screenings; students lack defensible AI architecture on resumes.
* **Overwhelming Time Commitment:** 40-hour online courses suffer < 5% completion rates during busy semesters.
* **Cloud Cost Anxiety:** Fear of incurring unexpected credit card charges on paid LLM APIs or complex AWS setups.

### 3. High-Leverage Workshop Proposition
* **Title:** *“Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes”*
* **Promise:** Go from idea to a working AI-powered mini project in one guided, 60-minute beginner-friendly sprint.
* **Deliverable:** Working software application, clean architecture comprehension, and zero paid cloud requirements.

## Slide 4: ₹2,000 Budget, Measurement & Optimization Rules

### 1. Disciplined Budget Allocation (₹2,000 Hard Cap)
* **₹500 (25%):** Campus Ambassador & Club Materials (Physical flyers, club noticeboard prints, lead rewards).
* **₹300 (15%):** Student-Community Creative Distribution (WhatsApp micro-graphics, Figma visual templates).
* **₹800 (40%):** Optional High-Intent Promotional Experiment (Search keywords for students seeking capstone projects).
* **₹400 (20%):** Contingency & Optimization Reserve (Emergency buffer to protect against channel decay).

### 2. Operational Decision Rules
* **The 70% Pacing Rule:** If cumulative acquisition drops below 70% of target by Day 4, freeze experimental spending, shift 100% effort to the top 2 highest-converting channels, and onboard 5 more student reps before using contingency.
* **The Conversion Friction Rule:** If a channel logs $\ge 20$ visits but $< 7.5\%$ conversion, immediately simplify form friction and align landing page headline with ad creative.

## Slide 5: Working Asset, Referral Mechanism, Assumptions & Risks

### 1. LaunchLoop AI Working Software Capabilities
* **Local NoSQL Document Database:** Fully persistent JSON document store with atomic writes, serialized mutex locks, and corruption recovery.
* **Collision-Resistant Referral Engine:** Instant unique code generation (`LL-XXXXX`), anti-self-referral validation, and privacy-safe public leaderboard.
* **Campaign Telemetry & Link Generator:** UTM link creation, visit tracking, and live conversion rate calculation without external cloud dependencies.
* **Explainable Growth Intelligence:** Rule-based deterministic Bayesian recommendation engine running with zero paid AI APIs.

### 2. Key Assumptions
* Campus ambassadors generate an average of 10-15 peer registrations across their respective departments.
* Beginner-friendly 60-minute practical project framing converts at 10-12% of tracked unique visits.

### 3. Risk Mitigation
* **WhatsApp Fatigue:** Hard limit of 2 high-value posts per group with actionable project architecture snippets.
* **Referral Gaming:** Strict server-side uniqueness enforcement, email normalization, and self-referral rejection.
